Giuseppina Andresini

Tenure-track Assistant Professor (RTD-B)
Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro

I work on artificial intelligence for cybersecurity and Earth observation. I combine deep learning, adversarial learning and explainable AI to build detectors of malware, network intrusions and review spam that are accurate, robust and transparent, and to map forest disturbance from satellite images.

Research

My research is carried out at the Knowledge Discovery & Data Engineering Lab (KDDE) of the Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro.

AI for cybersecurity

Machine learning and deep learning for malware detection (Windows PE and Android), network intrusion detection and review spam detection, including graph neural networks and vision transformers.

Adversarial learning and explainability

Training classifiers with adversarial examples, generating adversarial malware, and using explainable AI to make deep models both more accurate and more transparent.

Learning when data changes

Deep learning methods that cope with class imbalance and with shifts in the data distribution over time and space, in data streams and spatio-temporal data.

Earth observation

Change detection and semantic segmentation of satellite images, with a focus on mapping forest dieback caused by bark beetle outbreaks in Sentinel-2 data.
